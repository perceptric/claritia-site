// How it works

From an anonymous visit to a met rep.

One live model of your buyer. Bring in what you already have, watch who is on the site, and know who to call today.

  1. // step 01

    Visit

    A small script records each session: the company by network, the pages, and the active time.

  2. // step 02

    Match

    Forms, sign-ins and email clicks tie visits to people, each link labelled Verified, Asserted or Probable.

  3. // step 03

    Import

    Contacts and companies from your CRM, email tools, forms, billing and files — with consent and unsubscribes.

  4. // step 04

    Segment

    AND/OR rules over fields, activity, visits and consent. The count updates as you build.

  5. // step 05

    Score

    Fit and intent per account, with the signals and the sentence that explains why now.

  6. // step 06

    Engage

    Journeys and pages pick the next move and write from your own content. You approve before anything sends.

// Trust & privacy

Every link has proof. Every send has a basis.

Identity with proof

Every visit-to-person link is Verified, Asserted or Probable, shows its evidence, and can be undone.

Consent on record

Every consent keeps its wording, source and time. Imports read unsubscribes before a row lands.

Region rules, on their own

Claritia follows each visitor's region privacy rules by itself, so your data stays lawful in each place.

Erase on request

Remove a contact and their events, consents and links are gone, from the app or the API.
