Questions.

Does Claritia need my whole CRM?

No. Connect a CRM and we keep it in step, or start with a file, a form or the website script alone. Every source is independent, and the sync is two-way only where you switch it on.

What happens to a visitor who never fills a form?

They stay a device and a company, matched by network. We do not invent an email. When a form, a sign-in or an email click ties them to a person, the visit joins that record and the evidence is kept.

How is consent handled in the EU?

Consent is recorded per contact with its wording, source and date, and marketing only sends with a lawful basis for that region. Where a country needs opt-in, the form shows an unticked box.

Can I export everything?

Yes. Contacts, fields and activity export to CSV, and the API and MCP server read the same data with the same permissions. There is no lock-in.

What is live today, and what is not?

Website intelligence, accounts, contacts, leads, companies, segments, sources, warehouses, imports, the sync log, sending, forms and scoring are live. Journeys, pages, reports and the Brand Brain are in build.

Does it read my warehouse?

Yes, read-only. One saved SELECT, on your schedule, from Postgres, Redshift, MySQL, ClickHouse, BigQuery, Snowflake or Databricks. The query runs in a read-only transaction with a row limit.
