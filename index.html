Questions

Something else? hello@tryclaritia.com

Do I have to leave HubSpot?

No. Claritia syncs both ways with HubSpot, Salesforce and Omniscient. Use its CRM, or keep yours.

Can I keep my current forms?

Yes. HubSpot, Webflow, Framer, WordPress, Typeform or plain HTML. Add one line to your site and Claritia reads them.

How long does setup take?

About 15 minutes. Add the line to your site, connect your calendar in one click and pick who gets which leads.

Where does lead data come from?

Your own records first, then the website and data vendors. Every value shows its source.

Does the AI act on its own?

No. It only picks from the outcomes you set and shows its reason. Emails and live changes wait for your approval.

Who sends the emails?

You do, from your own domain. Claritia sets up the records and stops sending if complaints rise.

Which calendars work?

Google and Outlook, connected in one click. Meetings go out with Google Meet, Zoom or Teams links.
