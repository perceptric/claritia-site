How is Claritia priced?

Every plan is priced per seat, and a seat is a teammate people can book a meeting with. Free is $0 for one seat. Starter is $15, Pro $39 and Business $79 per seat a month, and paying yearly saves 20%. See every plan.

How long does setup take?

About 15 minutes. Add the form to your site, connect Google Calendar or Outlook in one click and pick who gets which leads.

How do I move from Typeform, Calendly or Chili Piper?

Keep the forms you have, or build new ones by chatting with AI or starting from a template. Google Forms import is built in, and Claritia can take answers and bookings from Typeform, Tally, Calendly and Cal.com while you switch.

How does routing decide who gets a lead?

You set rules from any answer, like team size, ZIP code or budget. Round robin with weights, caps and away days picks the next rep, and the routing log keeps the reason for every decision. Routing is on Pro and Business.

Does the AI send emails without me checking?

No. AI follow-ups are written from your own content and the lead's answers, and they wait for your approval before they send.

How does Claritia look after deliverability?

Follow-ups go out from your own domain on Pro and Business, or from your own Gmail or Outlook. Reminders and confirmations go out on their own, a day and an hour before each meeting.

Does it work with my CRM and calendar?

Yes. Google Calendar and Outlook work on every plan, HubSpot from Starter and Salesforce on Business, plus 70 more tools.

Will it work for my industry?

Yes. There are 435 form templates for SaaS, agencies, healthcare, real estate, education, fitness, legal and more, and you can describe any form you need.
