// How it works

From an anonymous visit to a booked meeting.

One platform follows the buyer the whole way. The agents move each account forward, and you approve anything that goes out.

  1. // step 01

    Visit

    A tiny script sees the session, the pages and the reading time. No personal data required.

  2. // step 02

    Match

    IP becomes a company, and identity stitching turns sessions into people you can act on.

  3. // step 03

    Segment

    Live segments over contacts, behavior and scores, with a count that updates as you build.

  4. // step 04

    Journey

    AI agents decide the branch and write each email from your Brand Brain. You approve.

  5. // step 05

    Handoff

    Ready leads go to the right rep in Omniscient or your CRM, with a brief and an SLA timer.

  6. // step 06

    Prove

    Attribution traces the AI answer, the page, the journey and the email to the won deal.

// Trust & control

The agent does the work. You keep the pen.

Approvals first

Every journey starts in review. Turn on auto mode only after 20 approved sends with few edits.

Every claim has a source

Emails and pages only state facts from your Brand Brain or the contact's own data. A verifier removes the rest.

Consent before sending

No marketing email goes out without a lawful basis for the contact's region. It's checked at send time.

No “I saw you were on…”

The agent never mentions tracking. A rule check blocks any message that does.

// Security & privacy

Your data and your buyers stay safe.

Sealed credentials

API keys, tokens and sending domains are encrypted on the server and never sent to the browser.

Isolated by workspace

Every table is locked to its workspace. One team can never see another's contacts.

Consent on record

Every consent keeps its wording, source and time, so you can prove the basis for any send.

Erase on request

Remove a contact and their events, consents and links are gone, from the app or the API.
