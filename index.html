How it works

Twenty seconds
from form to calendar.

Here is what happens when someone asks for a demo. You set it up once. Claritia runs it for every lead.

0:00Visit

Dana from Acme opens your pricing page. Claritia knows the company from the visit.

0:05Fill

Dana types a work email. Claritia already knows Acme's size and industry, so the form drops from 6 fields to 2.

0:15Qualify

Your rules pass. AI reads the “what are you looking for” answer and marks Dana qualified, with its reason.

0:16Route

Acme has no owner, so the router picks the US team and hands Dana to Sam, who has room this week.

0:20Book

Sam's open times show inside the form. Dana picks Thursday at 10. The invite lands on both calendars and Slack tells Sam, with a short brief.

Reminders go out the day before and the hour before. If Dana leaves without booking, Claritia emails Sam's times.

FAQ

Questions.

Do I have to leave HubSpot?

No. Claritia syncs both ways with HubSpot, Salesforce and Omniscient, and pushes leads to Pipedrive, Attio and Close. Use Claritia as your CRM or keep yours.

Can I keep my current forms?

Yes. Claritia works with HubSpot, Webflow, Marketo, Framer, Typeform and plain HTML forms. Routing and booking work the same on them.

Who counts as a host?

Anyone who takes meetings. People who build forms, edit workflows or read reports are free.

How long does setup take?

About 15 minutes. Sign up, connect your calendar, pick a form template and paste one line on your site. Your first test fill books a real meeting.

Where does lead data come from?

Your own records and CRM first, then data vendors in order. Every value shows its source and date, and a person's own edit always wins.

Does the AI act on its own?

AI qualify can only pick an outcome your team set up, and it shows its reason. Emails AI writes wait for your approval before they send.

Do I need a contract?

No. Plans are monthly. Pay yearly if you want 2 months free.
