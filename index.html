01 Brand

Your forms match your website.

Enter your website address. Claritia reads its fonts, colors and buttons, and applies them to every form and booking page.

  • Styles read from your live site
  • Applied to forms, booking pages and emails
  • Adjustable at any time

02 Forms

Describe a form and Claritia builds it.

Write what the form is for. Claritia drafts the questions, saves each answer to the right contact field and checks the form before it goes live.

  • Thirteen question types, multi-step and conditional logic
  • Work email checks and spam filtering
  • Embed on your site or share a link

03 Routing

Each lead reaches the right representative.

Set conditions on any answer or company detail. Claritia assigns the lead to a person or a team in rotation and records the reason.

  • Conditions on answers, company size, region and owner
  • Round robin that skips people who are away
  • Test a sample lead before you publish

04 Meetings

Meetings are booked inside the form.

After a submission, the assigned representative's open times appear in the form. The visitor picks a time and the invitation is sent.

  • Google Calendar and Outlook
  • Individual and dynamic meetings
  • Reschedule and cancel links in every invitation

05 CRM

Every person has one complete record.

Submissions, meetings and email activity are kept on one contact. Records stay in sync with HubSpot and Salesforce.

  • Two-way sync with HubSpot and Salesforce
  • Import from a CSV file
  • Segments and custom fields

06 Nurture

Follow-up runs on its own.

Workflows send emails, reminders and updates based on what each lead does. Lead scoring ranks contacts by fit and activity.

  • Meeting reminders that follow reschedules
  • Emails from your own domain, approved before sending
  • Scores based on fit and activity

Questions

Something else? hello@tryclaritia.com

Do I have to leave HubSpot?

No. Claritia syncs both ways with HubSpot, Salesforce and Omniscient. Use its CRM, or keep yours.

Can I keep my current forms?

Yes. HubSpot, Webflow, Framer, WordPress, Typeform or plain HTML. Add one line to your site and Claritia reads them.

How long does setup take?

About 15 minutes. Add the line to your site, connect your calendar in one click and pick who gets which leads.

Where does lead data come from?

Your own records first, then the website and data vendors. Every value shows its source.

Does the AI act on its own?

No. It only picks from the outcomes you set and shows its reason. Emails and live changes wait for your approval.

Who sends the emails?

You do, from your own domain. Claritia sets up the records and stops sending if complaints rise.

Which calendars work?

Google and Outlook, connected in one click. Meetings go out with Google Meet, Zoom or Teams links.
