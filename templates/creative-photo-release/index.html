Frequently asked questions

Something else? hello@tryclaritia.com

Can I customize the photo release form?

Yes. Add, remove and reorder questions, change the wording and options, and set which ones are required. Pick one of 18 styles or your own color. Tailor can also rewrite the wording for your business from one sentence.

What does the photo release form collect?

It asks for name and email, plus 7 more questions. You can add or remove any of them.

Is the photo release form mobile-friendly?

Yes. Every form fits phones, tablets and computers, and works with a keyboard and screen readers.

Is the photo release form template free?

Yes. Every form template is free on every plan, including the free one. Use it, change it and share it as often as you like.

Where do the answers go?

Each answer is saved in Claritia with the page and campaign the visitor came from. From there it can sync to HubSpot, Salesforce or Omniscient.

How do I put it on my website?

Share the link, or paste one line of code where the form should show. It fits any website, including Webflow, WordPress, Framer and plain HTML.

Does it ask for consent?

Yes. Visitors in the EU, the UK and other strict regions see an opt-in box; everyone else sees a short notice. Claritia saves the exact words each person saw.
