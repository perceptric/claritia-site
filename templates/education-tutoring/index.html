Questions

Something else? hello@tryclaritia.com

Is the tutoring request template free?

Yes. Every template is free on every plan, including the free one. Use it, change it and share it as often as you like.

Can I change the questions?

Yes. Add, remove and reorder questions, change the wording and options, set which questions are required, and show a question only when an earlier answer calls for it. Tailor can also rewrite the wording for your business from one sentence.

Where do the answers go?

Each answer becomes a contact in Claritia, with the company found from the work email and the page the visitor came from. From there it can sync to HubSpot, Salesforce or Omniscient.

How do I put it on my website?

Share the link, or paste one line of code where the form should show. It fits any website, including Webflow, WordPress, Framer and plain HTML.

Does it ask for consent?

Yes. Visitors in the EU, the UK and other strict regions see an opt-in box; everyone else sees a short notice. Claritia saves the exact words each person saw.
