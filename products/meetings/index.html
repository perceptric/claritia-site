    Questions

    Something else? hello@tryclaritia.com

    Which calendars work?

    Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook, connected in one click. Zoom connects for video links.

    Can I move from Calendly or Cal.com?

    Yes. Bookings from Calendly and Cal.com can come into Claritia while you switch over.

    What is a host?

    Anyone people can book. Each plan includes 1 host, and each extra host is a small monthly price. Everyone else on the team is free.

    Can guests pay to book?

    Yes, on Starter and up. Connect Stripe and set a price on the meeting type.
