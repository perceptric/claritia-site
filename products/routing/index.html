      Questions

      Something else? hello@tryclaritia.com

      Do I need a CRM to route?

      No. Rules read the form's answers. Connect HubSpot or Salesforce on Business to route known leads to their owner.

      Which plan has routing?

      Routing by answer, round robin and team pools are on Pro and up. Owner first and double round robin are on Business.

      What if nobody is free?

      The lead can pick a later time, and the team gets a reminder if a routed lead hasn't booked.

      Can I see why someone got a lead?

      Yes. Every meeting shows the rule that matched and why that person was picked.
