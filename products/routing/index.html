How it works

Set the rules once, route every lead

  1. 01

    Ask what you need to know

    Team size, country, budget or anything else, right in the form.

  2. 02

    Add rules in On submission

    If an answer is this, book that meeting or that person.

  3. 03

    Build your team

    Who takes turns, their weights, caps and away days.

  4. 04

    Leads book with the right person

    On the spot, with a Slack note to the host.
