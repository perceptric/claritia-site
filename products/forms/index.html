        Questions

        Something else? hello@tryclaritia.com

        Can I keep my current forms?

        Yes. Fills from HubSpot, Webflow, Typeform and Tally forms can come into Claritia, so they book and follow up the same way.

        Where do the answers go?

        Into Claritia first, then to the tools you connect: HubSpot, Salesforce, Google Sheets, Airtable, Notion, Slack, email lists and webhooks.

        How many forms can I make?

        As many as you like, on every plan. Plans differ by submissions a month: 250 on Free, 2,000 on Starter, 5,000 on Pro and unlimited on Business.

        Are the templates free?

        Yes. All 435 are free on every plan, including Free.
