What is an AI form builder?

An AI form builder is a form tool that drafts the form for you. You describe what you need in plain words, and the AI writes the questions and picks the field types. You then review the draft, change what you need and share the form.

The AI writes the first draft. How the form works once it's live still comes down to the form builder under it.

Typical features include:

  • A form drafted from a plain-language prompt.
  • Edits by chat after the first draft.
  • A form rebuilt from a PDF, a screenshot or an image.
  • Help with translations, formulas or answer choices.
  • AI help reading the answers, such as themes in open replies.
  • A set number of AI uses or credits on each plan.

How to choose an AI form builder

Most AI builders make a decent first draft. These five checks tell them apart:

  • What the AI really does. Read the AI features each vendor lists on its own site.
  • Edits after the first draft. A good AI builder lets you keep shaping the form.
  • The form under the AI. Logic and integrations still matter once the draft is done.
  • AI limits by plan. Check how many AI uses each plan includes.
  • Price. Look at what you pay for the form tool as a whole.

Tia, its AI form builder, works on every plan. The forms it makes can use conditional logic and book a meeting inside the form, so the lead goes from answers to a booked time in one step.

2. Typeform

Typeform is an online form builder for marketing, product, HR, and customer teams that want forms, surveys, and quizzes people actually finish. It builds conversational forms that adapt to each answer, can draft and brand a form from a written prompt, and accepts video and audio replies.

Responses can go to tools like HubSpot, Google Sheets, and Notion, take Stripe payments, book Calendly meetings, and feed AI summaries of themes and sentiment.

Its AI can also translate a form into more than 25 languages and ask a follow-up when a reply is vague. That gives you AI help before the form goes live and after.

Key features

  • AI form creation: Builds a form from a prompt, from the Basic plan.
  • Translation: Turns a form into more than 25 languages.
  • Clarify with AI: Asks follow-up questions when an open answer is vague.
  • Ask AI: Answers questions about your data and draws charts.
  • Import and brand matching: Imports Google Forms and matches your brand from a web address.

Pros

  • AI before and after launch: Help with building the form and with reading the answers.
  • Themes in open answers: AI finds patterns in long, open-ended replies.

Cons

  • Tight response caps: 100 responses a month on Basic.
  • AI spread across plans: Some AI features sit on specific plans, such as AI results analysis on Business.

Price and plans

  • Free: Yes, with a low response cap.
  • Basic: $28 a month, billed yearly.
  • Plus: $56 a month, billed yearly.
  • Business: $91 a month, billed yearly.

3. Jotform

Jotform is a drag and drop form builder for businesses, schools, nonprofits, and public agencies that want to collect data, payments, and signatures without writing code. Forms can use conditional logic, answer piping, prefilled fields, and translations, and they accept one-time or recurring payments through many gateways.

Submissions can become PDFs, flow into CRMs and spreadsheets, and be managed in related Jotform tools for tables, approval workflows, e-signatures, and reports. Its AI Form Generator creates a form from a text prompt, and you refine it by chatting with the AI, asking for new fields or colors.

Jotform says the generator is free to use, with form limits set by your plan. You get a free AI generator inside a form builder that has been around a long time.

Key features

  • AI Form Generator: Builds a form from a text prompt, free to use.
  • Chat edits: Change fields and design by chatting after the first draft.
  • Voice input: Speak your prompt instead of typing it.
  • AI Agents: On every plan, with limits by tier.

Pros

  • Free AI: The generator costs nothing to use.
  • Edits past the draft: Chat changes cover both fields and design.

Cons

  • Small free plan: 5 forms and 100 submissions a month.
  • Single user: Paid plans are for one user until Enterprise.

Price and plans

  • Starter: Free, with 5 forms and 100 submissions a month.
  • Bronze: $34 a month, billed yearly.
  • Silver: $39 a month, billed yearly.
  • Gold: $99 a month, billed yearly.

4. Fillout

Fillout is a form builder for teams that want branded forms, surveys, and quizzes connected to the tools they already use. You can describe a form for its AI to build or use drag and drop, then add logic, file uploads, payments, and signatures.

It also offers scheduling forms that sync with Google Calendar or Outlook, PDF generation, workflows, login forms that let people edit past answers, custom domains, and native integrations.

Its AI form builder is powered by Zite and can also start from an uploaded PDF. You can ask for changes in plain language, and nothing goes live until you publish. If you have old PDF forms to put online, this is a quick route.

Key features

  • Prompt or PDF: Builds a form from a description or an uploaded PDF.
  • Plain-language edits: Ask for changes in words, or use a design mode for hands-on changes.
  • Your brand on the draft: Applies your brand to the first draft.
  • Review before publish: Nothing goes live until you publish.

Pros

  • Big free plan: 1,000 responses a month for the forms themselves.
  • Old PDFs to forms: A quick way to bring PDF forms online.

Cons

  • Unclear AI limits: The AI builder page lists 50 AI credits a month free and leaves paid credit amounts unstated.
  • Free plan branding: Free forms show a Fillout badge.

Price and plans

  • Free: $0, with 1,000 responses a month.
  • Starter: $15 a month, billed yearly.
  • Pro: $40 a month, billed yearly.
  • Business: $75 a month, billed yearly.

5. Tally

Tally is a form builder for individuals and teams who want to make forms by typing, much like writing in a document. You insert blocks for contact details, file uploads, ratings, signatures, and Stripe payments, then add conditional logic, calculations, hidden fields, and answer piping.

Forms can be embedded or shown as popups, and submissions can go to Notion, Slack, Airtable, or Google Sheets. Tally is hosted in Europe and does not use cookie tracking.

Tally AI builds a form from a plain-language prompt, and inside a form you can click "Ask AI" to describe a change, which Tally says it makes without rewriting the whole form. It also rebuilds a form from a screenshot or PDF, and the free plan has no submission cap.

Key features

  • Prompt to form: Generates a form from plain language.
  • Ask AI: Targeted edits that leave the rest of the form alone.
  • Screenshot or PDF import: Recreates a form from an upload.
  • Any language: Understands prompts in any language and writes back in that language.
  • Your data stays out of training: Tally says your forms and submissions are never used to train AI models.

Pros

  • No submission cap: The free plan is unlimited within fair use.
  • Careful edits: Changes stay with the part you asked about.

Cons

  • Partial submissions cost more: They need Pro.
  • Fewer native integrations: Many teams use Zapier or Make.

Price and plans

  • Free: $0, unlimited within fair use.
  • Pro: $24 a month, billed monthly.
  • Business: $74 a month, billed monthly.

Yearly billing gives two months off.

6. Paperform

Paperform is an online form builder for small businesses and solo operators who want one form to capture details, take payment, and start the next step. Its editor works like a document, with a one question at a time mode, conditional logic, and spreadsheet style formulas for prices and scores.

Forms can take bookings and Stripe payments, send contracts for e-signature through Papersign, and pass submissions to CRMs, email tools, and spreadsheets through integrations or Stepper workflows. AI form creation and AI workflow building come on every plan, the free one included.

On Pro and up, an AI calculations assistant helps you build formulas, and AI report insights help you read results.

Key features

  • AI form creation: On every plan.
  • Calculations assistant: Helps you build formulas, on Pro and up.
  • AI report insights: Helps you read results, on Pro and up.
  • AI workflow building: On every plan.
  • Logic and appointments: Conditional logic and an appointment field on every plan.

Pros

  • AI on every plan: Form creation and workflow building come with the free plan too.
  • Help with the math: The calculations assistant helps you write formulas.

Cons

  • Tiny free plan: 30 submissions a month.
  • AI extras cost more: The AI tools for reports and calculations need a paid plan.

Price and plans

  • Free: 30 submissions a month.
  • Pro: $49 a month billed yearly, or $59 billed monthly.
  • Business: $124 a month billed yearly, or $149 billed monthly.

7. Zoho Forms

Zoho Forms is an online form builder for businesses that want to collect data and move it through review and approval steps without code.

It can build a form from a prompt, turn PDFs or photos of paper forms into online forms, and read business cards or IDs to fill in fields. Forms support many field types, subforms, payments, and multi-level approvals, a mobile app collects entries offline, and data can flow into Zoho CRM.

Its AI tools sit under the Zia assistant, which also suggests content and choices. AI use runs on credits, and you can buy more as an add-on. Teams with stacks of paper or PDF forms get the most from it.

Key features

  • AI forms: Zia, its assistant, builds forms with AI.
  • PDF and image to form: Plus smart scan for paper forms.
  • Suggestions: Zia suggests content and answer choices.
  • Translation: Translates your forms into other languages.
  • Extra AI credits: Sold as an add-on.

Pros

  • Many ways to start: Begin from a prompt, a PDF or an image.
  • More credits when you need them: Buy extra AI credits as an add-on.

Cons

  • Unclear AI limits: The pricing page leaves it unclear how many AI credits each plan gets.
  • Small free plan: 3 forms.

Price and plans

  • Free: 3 forms and 500 submissions.
  • Paid plans: See their pricing page.

8. Google Forms

Google Forms is the survey and form tool in Google Workspace, made for people and teams who want to gather answers from any device with little setup.

You pick question types, arrange them with drag and drop, add logic that shows questions based on earlier answers, or build quizzes that test knowledge. Forms can be shared by email, link, or embed. Responses appear as live charts and can be exported to Google Sheets for deeper analysis.

Its "Help me create a form" tool, powered by Gemini, drafts a form or quiz from your description and can pull context from your Docs, Sheets, Slides and PDFs. You review the draft before you create it. For a quick internal form on Google Workspace, that is often enough.

Key features

  • Help me create a form: Drafts a form or a quiz from a prompt.
  • Your files as context: Pulls from your Docs, Sheets, Slides and PDFs.
  • Review first: You check the draft before you create the form.
  • Google Sheets: Answers flow into Google Sheets.

Pros

  • Free to start: Google Forms itself is free for personal use.
  • Fits Google Workspace: The AI uses the Google files you already have.

Cons

  • AI needs a plan: The AI feature needs an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan.
  • Limited AI edits: Google says it can't edit an existing form or build forms with multiple sections.

Price and plans

  • Personal: Free.
  • Workspace: See their pricing page.

Which AI form builder should you pick?

  • If you want an AI-built form that books meetings with the right person, pick Claritia.
  • If you want AI to help read your answers as well as build the form, pick Typeform.
  • If you have old PDF forms to bring online, pick Fillout or Zoho Forms.
  • If you want AI edits on a free plan with no submission cap, pick Tally.
  • If you need a quick internal form and already pay for Google Workspace, use Google Forms.

Questions

What is an AI form builder?

An AI form builder creates a form from a short description. You type what you need, and the AI writes the questions and picks the field types. You then edit the draft before you share it.

Are AI form builders free?

Many have a free option. Claritia includes 100 AI credits on its free plan. Jotform says its AI generator is free to use.

Can an AI form builder turn a PDF into a form?

Some can. Fillout and Tally both list PDF uploads on their own pages, and Zoho Forms lists PDF to form. Google Forms can use a PDF as context for a draft.

Will the AI build conditional logic for me?

It depends on the tool. Most AI builders draft the questions first. In Claritia, you can add conditional logic and step skips to the form Tia builds on any plan.

What should I check before picking an AI form builder?

Look past the first draft. Check how many AI uses your plan includes and what happens to each answer after the form.
