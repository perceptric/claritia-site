So the right pick depends less on feature lists and more on what you sell. If you sell your time to clients, Acuity has more of what you need on every plan. If you book meetings with leads or partners, Calendly fits better.

## What is Calendly?

[Calendly](https://calendly.com/) is scheduling software for professionals and teams who want people to book meetings without trading emails. You connect your calendars, set hours, buffers, and daily limits, and share booking links for one-on-one, multi-host, or group meetings. It sends email and text reminders, offers routing forms that send website leads to the right teammate, and collects payments.

Newer tools include an AI assistant that finds times over email and a notetaker that writes meeting recaps. Reminders, payments and team meetings come on paid plans, with round robin and lead routing on Teams. The AI notetaker and email scheduling assistant are sold as add-ons.

## What is Acuity Scheduling?

[Acuity Scheduling](https://acuityscheduling.com/) is appointment scheduling software for service businesses such as salons, coaches, wellness providers, fitness studios, and tutors that want clients to book and pay on their own. Clients pick times on a branded booking page, fill out intake forms, and get automatic confirmations and reminders.

Owners can take deposits or full payments through Stripe, Square, or PayPal, sell packages, subscriptions, and gift certificates, run waitlists, and manage staff calendars across locations. Every plan includes custom client forms, payments, email reminders and time zone conversion. Higher plans add text reminders, the waitlist, packages, memberships and HIPAA support.

## Price and plans

The two tools price in different ways, so run the numbers for your team size.

| Calendly plan | Yearly | Monthly | Main limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 1 event type, 1 calendar connection |
| Standard | $10 a seat a month | $12 a seat a month | Unlimited event types, 6 calendars |
| Teams | $16 a seat a month | $20 a seat a month | Adds round robin and routing |
| Enterprise | From $15,000 a year | Yearly only | From 50 seats |

| Acuity plan | Yearly | Monthly | Calendars |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $16 a month | $20 a month | 1 |
| Standard | $27 a month | $34 a month | Up to 6 |
| Premium | $55 a month | $69 a month | Up to 36 |

Calendly charges for each person who hosts meetings. Acuity charges one price for the account, and each calendar can be a staff member, a room or a location. For one person, Calendly is cheaper and has a free plan.

For a small business with four staff, Acuity Standard at $27 a month billed yearly covers all of them, while Calendly Standard for four seats is $40 a month billed yearly.

## Payments, packages and memberships

Acuity takes payments on every plan through Stripe, Square, PayPal or Venmo, and you can ask for deposits. From Standard, you can sell appointment packages, memberships and gift certificates. If clients prepay for a set of sessions or a monthly plan, this is where Acuity stands out.

Calendly collects payments at booking through Stripe or PayPal from the Standard plan. Its paid plans now also list payment links, prepaid meeting packages and invoices. That handles simple paid calls. For businesses that sell many kinds of appointments, Acuity still has more.

## Intake forms and booking questions

Acuity includes custom client forms on every plan. You can ask for health history, preferences or anything else you need before the visit.

Calendly lets you add questions to the booking page. On Teams, it adds forms that screen invitees and route leads based on their answers, from Calendly's own forms or from your HubSpot, Pardot or Marketo forms. That's built for qualifying sales leads. Acuity's client forms suit patient and client intake better.

## Reminders and no-shows

Acuity sends confirmation, reminder and follow-up emails on every plan. Text reminders start on Standard, and Acuity notes that they depend on your country. Standard also adds a waitlist for full time slots.

Calendly's free plan doesn't include automated reminders. Standard adds automations and reminders, plus a cancellation policy you can show to invitees.

## Teams and routing

This is Calendly's strongest area. Teams adds round robin, which shares meetings across teammates, and routing forms that send each lead to the right person. It can route by the owner set in HubSpot, and it sends meetings to Salesforce. Routing by Salesforce lookup sits on Enterprise.

Acuity handles staff through calendars, and the plan you pick sets how many you get. Premium adds multiple time zones for staff and locations. Its pricing page doesn't list round robin or lead routing. It's made for clients booking a service, not for a sales team sharing leads.

## Integrations

Calendly connects Google, Outlook and Microsoft calendars, Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams. Standard adds HubSpot, Mailchimp, Zapier, Google Analytics and Meta Pixel. Teams adds Salesforce and Marketo.

Acuity lists Google Meet, Zoom and GoToMeeting for online sessions on every plan. Premium adds a custom API and CSS for developers.

## Pros and cons

### Calendly

Pros:

- **Free plan:** One event type and one calendar for $0.
- **Team routing:** Round robin and routing forms on Teams.
- **CRM links:** HubSpot from Standard, Salesforce from Teams.
- **Payments from Standard:** Stripe and PayPal at booking.

Cons:

- **Small free plan:** One event type and no automated reminders.
- **Per seat:** Costs rise with each host.
- **Routing costs more:** Routing forms need Teams at $16 a seat billed yearly.

### Acuity Scheduling

Pros:

- **Built for appointments:** Intake forms and payments on every plan.
- **Many ways to pay:** Stripe, Square, PayPal and Venmo.
- **Packages and memberships:** From Standard.
- **HIPAA support:** A signed BAA on Premium.

Cons:

- **No free plan:** Only a 7-day trial.
- **One calendar on Starter:** A second staff member means Standard.
- **Less for sales teams:** No round robin or lead routing listed.

- **Pick Calendly Teams** if you need to share leads across a sales team with round robin and routing.
- **Pick Acuity Premium** if you run a health practice and need a signed HIPAA agreement.

## Questions

### Is Acuity better than Calendly?

For appointment businesses, often yes. Acuity includes intake forms and payments on every plan, plus packages and memberships from Standard. For sales teams and free use, Calendly fits better.

### Does Acuity Scheduling have a free plan?

No. Acuity offers a 7-day free trial with no credit card needed. Its paid plans start at $16 a month billed yearly.

### Is Calendly cheaper than Acuity?

For one person, yes. Calendly has a free plan and Standard costs $10 a seat a month billed yearly. For a business with several staff, Acuity can cost less, since Standard covers up to 6 calendars for $27 a month billed yearly.

### Can Calendly take payments like Acuity?

Yes, on paid plans. Calendly collects payments through Stripe or PayPal from Standard. Acuity takes Stripe, Square, PayPal and Venmo on every plan.

### Which is better for therapists, Calendly or Acuity?

Acuity is usually the better fit. It has intake forms on every plan, and Premium includes a signed BAA for HIPAA compliance.
