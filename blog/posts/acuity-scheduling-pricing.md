## Acuity Scheduling pricing: how much does it cost?

[Acuity Scheduling](https://acuityscheduling.com/) is appointment scheduling software for service businesses such as salons, coaches, wellness providers, fitness studios, and tutors that want clients to book and pay on their own. Clients pick times on a branded booking page, fill out intake forms, and get automatic confirmations and reminders.

Owners can take deposits or full payments through Stripe, Square, or PayPal, sell packages, subscriptions, and gift certificates, run waitlists, and manage staff calendars across locations. Acuity has three self-serve plans, Starter, Standard and Premium, plus an Enterprise offer through its sales team.

## Acuity Starter

**Who it fits:** A solo service provider with one calendar, like a personal trainer, a tutor or a coach working alone.

**What it includes:** One calendar, unlimited services and appointments, intake forms, payments and deposits, email confirmations and reminders, and video links for online sessions. You can show your logo and set your scheduler's colors and copy.

**Limits:** One calendar means one person or one location. You can't send text reminders, run a waitlist, or sell packages, memberships, subscriptions or gift certificates. The "Powered by Acuity Scheduling" footer stays on your page.

**Price:** $16 a month billed yearly, or $20 billed monthly.

## Acuity Standard

**Who it fits:** A small business with a few staff members or rooms, such as a salon with four chairs or a studio with two teachers.

**What it adds:** Up to 6 calendars, text message reminders, a waitlist that tells clients when a full time opens up, appointment packages with payment plans, and the option to sell subscriptions, memberships and gift certificates.

**Limits:** Text reminders depend on your country, and Acuity asks you to check its list of where they work. The Acuity footer is still on your page. There's no signed HIPAA agreement, no AI Booking Assistant and no custom CSS or API.

**Price:** $27 a month billed yearly, or $34 billed monthly.

## Acuity Premium

**Who it fits:** Health and wellness practices, businesses with several locations, and anyone who wants the booking page to carry only their brand.

**What it adds:** Up to 36 calendars, the AI Booking Assistant (a chatbot that helps clients book), different time zones for each staff member or location, and the option to hide the "Powered by Acuity Scheduling" footer. It also lets you sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) for HIPAA compliance and adds custom CSS and API access for developers.

**Limits:** 36 calendars is the cap. Past that, you need Enterprise.

**Price:** $55 a month billed yearly, or $69 billed monthly.

## Acuity Enterprise

**Who it fits:** Chains and groups with many locations.

**What it adds:** Acuity lists unlimited calendars, VIP account management, unlimited scheduling instances, more APIs and a central dashboard to manage them all.

**Limits:** There's no public price. You contact Acuity's Enterprise team for a quote.

## What the free trial and Starter leave out

Acuity has no free plan, so the real question is what you miss on the cheapest plan. On Starter, you go without:

- **A second calendar.** One staff member or one location only. A second person means Standard.
- **Text reminders.** Starter sends email reminders only. Texts start on Standard.
- **A waitlist.** Clients can't join a list for a full time slot until Standard.
- **Packages and memberships.** Selling a 5-session pack, a monthly membership or a gift certificate needs Standard.
- **Your brand alone.** The Acuity footer stays on every plan below Premium.
- **HIPAA support.** A signed BAA is only on Premium.

## Extra costs to watch

Acuity says there are no set-up, cancellation or support fees. These are the costs that still add up:

- **The calendar caps.** Plans jump at 1, 6 and 36 calendars. Hiring a seventh staff member moves you from $27 to $55 a month on yearly billing.
- **Monthly billing.** Paying month to month adds $4 a month on Starter, $7 on Standard and $14 on Premium.
- **Taxes.** Listed prices don't include tax.
- **Card fees.** Payments run through your own Stripe, Square, PayPal or Venmo account, so that provider's processing fees apply on each payment.
- **Features you need for one reason.** A health practice that needs a BAA, or a business that wants the footer gone, has to buy Premium even with one calendar.
- **Other tools for sales work.** Acuity's integrations page lists Zoho CRM under CRM, plus Zapier. If you use HubSpot or Salesforce, you may pay for a Zapier plan as well.
- **The billing date.** Your billing cycle starts on the day you sign up, and invoices follow that date each month.

## Is Acuity Scheduling worth it?

For many appointment businesses, yes. Intake forms, payments and deposits come on every plan, which some booking tools charge more for. Clients get a clean booking page in their own time zone, and you get reminders without building anything.

Standard is the plan most small businesses end up on. It covers up to 6 staff or rooms, text reminders, a waitlist, packages and memberships for $27 a month billed yearly. Starter is fine for one person who only needs email reminders.

Premium makes sense for health practices that need a signed BAA, or for businesses that care about removing the Acuity footer. For everyone else, it's a big step up for features you may not use.

Acuity is a weaker fit for teams that book meetings with leads. Its pricing page doesn't list round robin or lead routing, and HubSpot and Salesforce aren't in its CRM list. In that case, compare it with Calendly in our [Calendly vs Acuity](/blog/calendly-vs-acuity) guide.

## Questions

### Is Acuity Scheduling free?

No. Acuity has no free plan. It offers a 7-day free trial of any plan with no credit card needed, and paid plans start at $16 a month billed yearly.

### How much is Acuity Scheduling a month?

Billed monthly, Starter is $20, Standard is $34 and Premium is $69. Billed yearly, they cost $16, $27 and $55 a month. Prices are in US dollars and don't include tax.

### Does Acuity Scheduling charge per user?

No. Acuity charges per account, and each plan includes a set number of calendars: 1 on Starter, up to 6 on Standard and up to 36 on Premium. A calendar can be a staff member or a location.

### What's the difference between Acuity Standard and Premium?

Premium raises the calendar limit from 6 to 36. It also adds the AI Booking Assistant, time zones per staff member or location, hiding the Acuity footer, a signed HIPAA agreement, and custom CSS and API access.

### Which Acuity plan has text reminders?

Text message reminders start on Standard, at $27 a month billed yearly. Acuity notes that they aren't available in every country.

### Is Acuity Scheduling HIPAA compliant?

On Premium, you can sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) for HIPAA compliance. Starter and Standard don't include it.
