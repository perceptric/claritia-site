Fillout pricing is built around responses, not people. Every plan, even the free one, lets you add as many teammates and forms as you like. What changes from plan to plan is how many responses you get each month, how much Fillout branding shows, and a set of features like partial submissions and a custom domain.

That makes Fillout cheaper than most form builders for a team, but it also means the response count and a few key features decide your plan. This page lays out what each Fillout plan costs, what it includes and where the extra costs come from.

## Is Fillout free?

Yes. Fillout has a Free plan that it calls free forever. You get unlimited forms, unlimited seats and 1,000 responses a month across your organization. There's no card needed.

The free plan is unusually full. Fillout lists multi-page forms, conditional logic, calculations and scoring, answer piping, pre-fill and hidden fields, payments, scheduling forms, PDF generation, unlimited file uploads and Fillout Workflows on it.

Most integrations come free too, including Airtable, Google Sheets, Notion, HubSpot, Mailchimp and webhooks, plus CSV export and API access.

The limits are elsewhere. Free forms carry a Fillout badge, have no CAPTCHA, and can't use custom endings, a redirect after submit, your logo or login forms. File uploads are capped at 20 MB each.

## Fillout Free

**Who it fits:** Individuals and teams who need working forms with logic, payments or booking, and fewer than 1,000 responses a month.

**What it includes:** Unlimited forms, questions and seats, 1,000 responses a month, more than 50 field types, conditional logic, calculations, hidden fields, resume in progress forms, payments, scheduling forms, PDF generation, workflows, most integrations and API access.

**Limits:** A "Made with Fillout" badge on every page, no CAPTCHA, no premium field types, no login forms, no custom themes, no custom endings and no redirect on completion. Files are capped at 20 MB.

## Fillout Starter

**Who it fits:** Small businesses that want forms that look more like their own and a bit more volume.

**What it adds:** 2,000 responses a month, all question types, custom endings and multiple endings, redirect on completion, login forms, CAPTCHA, custom themes and your logo. It also adds emails to the person who filled in the form, collecting signatures and Calendly scheduling.

**Limits:** Branding is lighter but still there. Fillout's help center says the "Made with Fillout" button shows as light branding on Starter, and Fillout's name stays in email notifications. No custom fonts, CSS or favicon.

**Price:** $15 a month billed yearly, or $19 billed monthly.

## Fillout Pro

**Who it fits:** Teams that want forms with no Fillout name on them at all.

**What it adds:** 5,000 responses a month, removal of all branding, custom share links and link previews, custom fonts and favicon, custom CSS, white-label emails and a setting to prevent duplicate submissions. Fillout's name also comes off scheduling emails and calendar events.

**Limits:** No partial submissions, no drop-off rates, no form analytics or Meta Pixel, and no custom domain. Those start on Business.

**Price:** $40 a month billed yearly, or $49 billed monthly.

## Fillout Business

**Who it fits:** Marketing teams running lead forms with real traffic.

**What it adds:** Unlimited responses under Fillout's fair use policy, form analytics and conversion tracking, Meta Pixel, drop-off rates, a view of partial submissions, a custom domain, custom code and HTML blocks, pre-fetching data into a form, files of 1 GB or more and priority support.

**Limits:** Salesforce, granular permissions and enforced two-factor sign in for all users are on the Team bundle and Enterprise.

**Price:** $75 a month billed yearly, or $89 billed monthly.

## Team bundle and Enterprise

**The Team bundle** is $250 a month billed yearly, or $3,000 a year. It's an all-in-one plan for forms, apps and databases built on Zite. Fillout lists more than 800 credits a month, unlimited users and workspaces, 1,000,000 records, 250,000 workflow runs a month, roles, granular permissions, unlimited brand kits and enforced two-factor sign in. It also unlocks the Salesforce integration.

**Enterprise** has a custom price through Fillout's sales team. It adds single sign-on (SSO), audit logs, unlimited workflow runs, unlimited revision history, dedicated support, custom agreements and SLAs, and the option to store files only in your own storage.

## What the free plan leaves out

Fillout's free plan covers more than most. What it lacks is mostly polish and data on who didn't finish:

- **Your own brand.** Free forms show a Fillout badge. Lighter branding starts on Starter, and no branding starts on Pro.
- **Spam protection.** CAPTCHA starts on Starter.
- **Custom endings and redirects.** Sending people to your own thank-you page needs Starter.
- **Login forms and signatures.** Both start on Starter.
- **Partial submissions.** Seeing answers from people who left early needs Business.
- **Drop-off rates and Meta Pixel.** Both need Business.
- **A custom domain.** Hosting forms on your own URL needs Business.
- **Salesforce.** It isn't on any of the four main plans. It needs the Team bundle or Enterprise.

## Extra costs to watch

The plan price is most of what you pay, but watch for these:

- **Going over your response limit.** Fillout says your forms keep collecting responses. If you go over often, it asks you to upgrade, and if you keep going over it may limit or suspend some features.
- **Test responses count.** Answers you submit yourself through the live link count toward the limit. Tests in preview mode don't count, and neither do partial submissions.
- **The count resets on the 1st.** Limits run per calendar month in UTC, not per billing cycle.
- **The Conversion Kit.** Form abandonment recovery, drop-off alerts, lead enrichment (300 a month), email validation, address autocomplete and lead routing by company data are a $100 a month add-on, and it needs the Business plan. With Business billed yearly, that comes to $175 a month.
- **Salesforce costs a big jump.** Going from Business at $75 a month to the Team bundle at $250 a month billed yearly is the only self-serve way to get it.
- **Monthly billing.** Paying month to month adds $4 a month on Starter, $9 on Pro and $14 on Business.

Fillout says you can cancel at any time. It also offers discounts for nonprofits, students, startups and schools if you apply.

## Is Fillout worth it?

For most small teams, yes. The free plan covers more than most form builders' free plans, and 1,000 responses a month goes a long way. Because seats are unlimited on every plan, a whole team can work on forms without raising the bill.

Starter is a good step for $15 a month billed yearly if you want spam protection, your logo and custom endings. Pro at $40 is the plan to pick when the Fillout name must come off completely.

Business at $75 a month billed yearly is worth it when you run lead forms and need partial submissions, drop-off data and your own domain. Add the Conversion Kit only if you'll use enrichment and abandonment recovery, since it doubles the price.

Fillout is a weaker fit if you need Salesforce on a budget, or if every form should end with a meeting booked with the right rep. It has scheduling forms, but sending each lead to a person by their answers and following up until they book takes more setup.

## Questions

### Is Fillout free?

Yes. Fillout's Free plan has no time limit and includes unlimited forms, unlimited seats and 1,000 responses a month, with logic, payments, scheduling forms and most integrations.

### How much does Fillout cost per month?

Billed monthly, Starter is $19, Pro is $49 and Business is $89. Billed yearly, they cost $15, $40 and $75 a month. The Team bundle is $250 a month billed yearly.

### Does Fillout charge per user?

No. Every Fillout plan, including Free, has unlimited seats. You pay one flat fee for the plan, and the response limit is shared across your forms.

### What happens if you go over your Fillout response limit?

Your forms keep taking responses. If you go over often, Fillout asks you to upgrade, and it may limit some features if you keep going over. The count resets on the 1st of each month.

### Which Fillout plan removes the branding?

Pro and every plan above it remove all Fillout branding. Starter keeps light branding, and Free shows the full badge.

### Does Fillout integrate with Salesforce?

Yes, but only on the Team bundle and Enterprise. HubSpot, Airtable, Google Sheets, Notion and most other integrations are on the free plan.
