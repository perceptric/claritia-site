## Is Jotform free?

Yes. Jotform's free plan is called Starter, and it has no time limit. It includes 5 forms, 100 submissions a month, 100 MB of file space and 10,000 form views a month, for one user. Jotform says the free plan includes all of its standard features, such as templates, widgets and payment integrations.

The limits are what push people to pay. Your forms show Jotform branding, you can keep only 500 submissions in total, and you can take just 10 payments a month. Once you hit a cap, you need a paid plan to raise it.

## Jotform pricing: how much does Jotform cost?

[Jotform](https://www.jotform.com/) has five plans: Starter (free), Bronze, Silver, Gold and Enterprise. Each step up raises the same set of limits. Prices are per account, and each plan below Enterprise covers one user.

## Jotform Starter (free)

**Who it fits:** Someone testing Jotform, or a person with one or two small forms, like a contact form or a club sign-up.

**What it includes:** 5 forms, 100 submissions a month, 100 MB of space, 10,000 views a month, up to 100 fields a form, and 10 payments and 10 signed documents a month. Templates, widgets and payment integrations are part of the free plan.

**Limits:** Jotform branding shows on your forms and signed documents. You can store only 500 submissions in total, so a busy form fills up fast. HIPAA features aren't available.

## Jotform Bronze

**Who it fits:** A freelancer or a small business with a handful of steady forms.

**What it adds:** 25 forms, 1,000 submissions a month, 1 GB of space, 100,000 views a month, 10,000 stored submissions, 250 fields a form, and 100 payments and 100 signed documents a month. Jotform branding is removed.

**Limits:** Still one user and no HIPAA features. The 25-form cap can come up quickly if you make a form for each event or client.

**Price:** $34 a month billed yearly, or $39 billed monthly.

## Jotform Silver

**Who it fits:** A growing business with more forms or more traffic. Jotform marks it as the best value.

**What it adds:** 50 forms, 2,500 submissions a month, 10 GB of space, 1,000,000 views a month, 25,000 stored submissions, 500 fields a form, and 250 payments and 250 signed documents a month.

**Limits:** One user and no HIPAA features.

**Price:** $39 a month billed yearly, or $49 billed monthly. Billed yearly, Silver costs only $5 a month more than Bronze, with double the forms and more than double the submissions.

## Jotform Gold

**Who it fits:** High-volume users and health businesses that need HIPAA features.

**What it adds:** 100 forms, 10,000 submissions a month, 100 GB of space, 10,000,000 views a month, 100,000 stored submissions, 1,000 fields a form, and 1,000 payments and 1,000 signed documents a month. Gold is the only plan below Enterprise with HIPAA features.

**Limits:** It's still a single-user plan, and the jump from Silver is large.

**Price:** $99 a month billed yearly, or $129 billed monthly.

## Jotform Enterprise

**Who it fits:** Larger organizations that need many users, single sign-on or a set data region.

**What it adds:** Unlimited forms and submissions, a platform for many users with user management, single sign-on (SSO), service level agreements, data residency, your own branding and domain, HIPAA features, dedicated support and professional services.

**Limits:** There's no public price. You get a quote from Jotform's sales team.

## What the free plan leaves out

Jotform's free plan has most features, so what it leaves out is mostly room to grow:

- **More forms.** Five forms is a low ceiling if you build a new form for each use.
- **More submissions.** 100 a month suits a contact form. A lead form with real traffic will pass it.
- **Long-term storage.** Only 500 submissions are stored in total across your account.
- **Payments at volume.** You can take 10 payments a month on the free plan.
- **Your own brand.** Jotform branding stays on forms and signed documents until you pay.
- **HIPAA features.** These need Gold or Enterprise.
- **A second user.** No plan below Enterprise adds more users.

## Extra costs to watch

These costs aren't on the price tag, but they shape what you pay:

- **One user per plan.** If two or three people build forms, each one needs a separate account, or you move to Enterprise.
- **Limits that move together.** The pricing page lists no add-ons for a single limit. If you only need more forms, you move up a plan and pay for all of its limits.
- **Monthly billing.** Paying monthly costs $5 more a month on Bronze, $10 more on Silver and $30 more on Gold.
- **HIPAA means Gold.** If you collect health data, the lowest plan with HIPAA features is $99 a month billed yearly.
- **Stored submission caps.** Busy forms can reach the total stored limit before the monthly one, especially on the free plan.

On the plus side, Jotform lets you cancel at any time and refunds a payment in full if you cancel within 30 days. Nonprofits and schools get 50% off Bronze, Silver and Gold, and 30% off Enterprise.

## Is Jotform worth it?

Jotform is worth it if you need a lot of field types and widgets in one place, or if you work in health and need HIPAA features without a sales call. Its free plan is also good for trying things out, since Jotform includes its standard features on it.

It's harder to justify for teams. Every plan below Enterprise is for one user, so a marketing team that shares form work either shares a login or talks to sales. The form caps can also catch you out if you build many small forms.

For a single person, Silver billed yearly is often the best deal, at $5 a month more than Bronze.
