Cal.com and Calendly do the same core job. You share a link, people pick a time, and the meeting lands on your calendar with a video link. Both have round robin and routing forms for teams. Both connect to HubSpot and Salesforce. From the outside, they look close.

The real differences sit in the plans. Cal.com gives a single user far more for free, costs less per user for teams, and lets you host the code yourself. Calendly has a smaller free plan but a long list of extras for sales teams and large companies. This guide walks through where each one wins so you can pick in a few minutes.

## What is Cal.com?

[Cal.com](https://cal.com/) is scheduling software for individuals and companies that want booking links they can shape around their own process. It syncs calendars, detects time zones, sends reminders, and runs video calls through Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, or its own Cal Video. Teams can use round robin, collective events where every host must be free, routing forms, and workflows, plus admin tools such as SAML SSO and audit logs. Its source code is on GitHub, with guides for hosting it yourself, and you can also use the hosted version at cal.com. The free plan is generous for one person, and paid plans add teams, round robin, routing forms and, at the top end, compliance features such as SOC 2 and HIPAA.

[![Cal.com homepage](/assets/blog/tools/cal-com.webp)](https://cal.com/)

## What is Calendly?

[Calendly](https://calendly.com/) is scheduling software for professionals and teams who want people to book meetings without trading emails. You connect your calendars, set hours, buffers, and daily limits, and share booking links for one-on-one, multi-host, or group meetings. It sends email and text reminders, offers routing forms that send website leads to the right teammate, and collects payments. Newer tools include an AI assistant that finds times over email and a notetaker that writes meeting recaps. Reminders, payments and team meetings come on paid plans, and the Teams plan adds round robin and lead routing. The notetaker and the email assistant, called Callie, are sold as add-ons.

## Free plans

This is the biggest gap between the two.

Cal.com's free plan is for one user, with unlimited links and calendar connections. It includes email and SMS notifications, Stripe and PayPal payments, two-way Salesforce and HubSpot sync, and a one-click import of your Calendly events. It also has a mobile app and a browser extension.

Calendly's free plan includes one event type and one calendar connection, with one-on-one meetings on a booking page you can customize. You can host unlimited meetings, but you can't add a second kind of meeting, and automated reminders need Standard.

For a solo user who wants a free tool, Cal.com wins by a wide margin.

## Team pricing and plans

| Plan level | Cal.com | Calendly |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 for 1 user | $0, 1 event type |
| Paid for one person | The free plan covers one user | Standard, $10 a seat a month billed yearly ($12 monthly) |
| Teams | $12 a user a month billed yearly ($16 monthly) | $16 a seat a month billed yearly ($20 monthly) |
| Larger companies | Organizations, $28 a user a month billed yearly | Enterprise, from $15,000 a year and 50 seats |

Cal.com also has an Enterprise plan with custom, yearly pricing. For a team of five on yearly billing, Cal.com Teams is $60 a month and Calendly Teams is $80 a month. Cal.com Teams comes with a 14-day free trial.

## Round robin and routing

Both tools put round robin and routing forms on their team plans.

Cal.com Teams includes round robin, collective events where everyone must be free, managed event types, and routing forms. Its Organizations plan adds routing by member attributes and custom variables, plus sub-teams.

Calendly Teams includes round robin, centrally managed event types, and forms that screen invitees and route leads. A strong point is that it can also route from your existing HubSpot, Pardot or Marketo forms. It can route by the owner set in HubSpot on Teams. Routing by Salesforce lookup sits on Enterprise.

If your leads come through Marketo or Pardot forms, Calendly has the edge. If you want routing at the lowest price, Cal.com does.

## CRM and integrations

Cal.com says it integrates with more than 100 apps, and two-way Salesforce and HubSpot sync is on the free plan.

Calendly connects HubSpot, Mailchimp and Zapier from Standard. Teams adds Salesforce and Marketo, and Enterprise adds Microsoft Dynamics. It also lists Google Analytics and Meta Pixel for tracking bookings from Standard.

Both connect Google and Outlook calendars, plus Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams.

## Hosting, security and control

Cal.com is the only one of the two you can host yourself. That matters to teams that want their booking data on their own servers, or developers who want to build on the code. Its Organizations plan lists SOC 2, HIPAA and ISO 27001 compliance, SAML SSO and SCIM.

Calendly is hosted only. SSO is an optional add-on on Teams, and Enterprise adds SAML, domain control, audit logs and a data deletion API.

## Pros and cons

### Cal.com

Pros:

- **Big free plan:** Unlimited links and calendars, payments and CRM sync for one user.
- **Lower team price:** $12 a user a month billed yearly.
- **Open source:** You can host it yourself.
- **Easy move:** One-click import of Calendly events.

Cons:

- **Self-hosting takes work:** Someone has to run and update it.
- **Advanced routing costs more:** Attribute-based routing needs Organizations.
- **More settings:** The long list of options takes time to learn.

### Calendly

Pros:

- **Routing from your forms:** Works with HubSpot, Pardot and Marketo forms on Teams.
- **AI add-ons:** Notetaker and Callie for Standard and Teams.
- **Enterprise depth:** Salesforce lookup routing, Dynamics and audit logs.
- **Payments tools:** Payment links, packages and invoices on paid plans.

Cons:

- **Small free plan:** One event type and one calendar.
- **Higher team price:** $16 a seat a month billed yearly.
- **Salesforce on Teams:** CRM sync costs more than on Cal.com.

## Which should you pick?

- **Pick Cal.com** if you're one person and want a free tool that does almost everything.
- **Pick Cal.com** if you want team scheduling at a lower price, or you need to host the code yourself.
- **Pick Calendly** if your leads come in through HubSpot, Pardot or Marketo forms and you want them routed on the spot.
- **Pick Calendly** if you're a large company that wants enterprise controls and a dedicated account team.

## Questions

### Is Cal.com better than Calendly?

For a single user or a team that wants a lower price, Cal.com usually is. For teams that route leads from HubSpot, Pardot or Marketo forms, or large companies that want enterprise controls, Calendly can fit better.

### Is Cal.com really free?

Yes. Cal.com's free plan is for one user and includes unlimited links and calendars, payments and HubSpot and Salesforce sync. Team features need a paid plan.

### Is Cal.com cheaper than Calendly?

Yes, for teams. Cal.com Teams is $12 a user a month billed yearly, and Calendly Teams is $16 a seat a month billed yearly.

### Can I move from Calendly to Cal.com?

Yes. Cal.com lists a one-click import of Calendly events on its free plan.

### Do Cal.com and Calendly both have round robin?

Yes. Both put round robin on their team plans, along with routing forms.
