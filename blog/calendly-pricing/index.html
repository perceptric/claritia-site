Calendly Pricing 2026: Every Plan, Limit and Hidden Cost

Calendly pricing for 2026, plan by plan. See what the free plan includes, what Standard, Teams and Enterprise cost per seat, and the extra costs to plan for.

Calendly pricing looks simple at first: a free plan, two paid plans and Enterprise. Then you notice that every price is per seat, that the yearly and monthly prices differ, and that the AI features now sit on separate Plus plans. A team of five can pay very different amounts depending on which boxes they tick.

If you're choosing a plan, or trying to work out why your bill went up, you need the real numbers and the limits behind each one. That's what this page gives you.

Below, we walk through each Calendly plan, what the free plan leaves out, the costs that are easy to miss, and when another tool fits better.

Main points

  • Yes, Calendly is free. The Free plan has no time limit and gives you one event type, one calendar and unlimited one-on-one meetings.
  • Paid plans are per seat: Standard is $10 a seat a month billed yearly ($12 monthly), and Teams is $16 billed yearly ($20 monthly).
  • The AI notetaker and the Callie email assistant cost extra through Standard Plus ($18 yearly) and Teams Plus ($24 yearly).
  • Round robin, routing forms and Salesforce start on Teams. Enterprise starts at $15,000 a year for 50 seats.
  • If you want forms and booking in one tool, Claritia books the meeting inside the form, from $0.

Is Calendly free?

Yes. Calendly has a Free plan that you can use for as long as you like, with no card. It covers one person with one event type, one connected calendar and as many one-on-one meetings as people book. You get a booking page you can customize, the mobile app and the browser extension.

The catch is the word "one." One event type means one kind of meeting, like a 30-minute call. One calendar means Calendly checks a single calendar for conflicts. Reminders, payments, group meetings and integrations like HubSpot all need a paid plan.

Calendly pricing: how much does Calendly cost?

Calendly has four main plans: Free, Standard, Teams and Enterprise. Standard and Teams each also come in a Plus version that adds AI features. Every paid plan is priced per seat. A seat is a person who connects a calendar and hosts meetings. The people who book with you don't need a seat or an account.

Calendly Free

Who it fits: One person who needs a single booking link, such as a freelancer or a founder taking intro calls.

What it includes: One event type, one calendar connection, unlimited one-on-one meetings, a booking page you can brand, meeting polls and one-off meeting links, video links for Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, the mobile app, the browser extension and 24/7 email support.

Limits: You can't create a second meeting type, check more than one calendar, send automatic reminders, take payments or host group meetings. Calendly branding stays on your page.

Calendly Standard

Who it fits: A solo professional or a small team where each person books their own meetings.

What it adds: Unlimited event types and up to 6 connected calendars. You also get automatic reminders and follow-ups, group and collective meeting types, Stripe and PayPal payments, and connections to HubSpot, Mailchimp and Zapier. You can remove the Calendly branding, use custom confirmation pages, track bookings with Google Analytics and Meta Pixel, and get 24/7 chat support.

Limits: No round robin, no routing forms and no Salesforce. If several people share incoming leads, you'll need Teams.

Price: $10 a seat a month billed yearly, or $12 billed monthly.

Calendly Teams

Who it fits: Sales, success and recruiting teams that share meetings across people.

What it adds: Round robin to spread meetings across the team, forms that screen and route leads to the right person, and event types that admins manage for everyone. It also connects to Salesforce, Marketo and Pardot, can route by HubSpot owner, and lets you organize people into groups with their own admins.

Limits: Single sign-on (SSO) is an optional add-on here. Routing by Salesforce lookup and Microsoft Dynamics 365 are only on Enterprise.

Price: $16 a seat a month billed yearly, or $20 billed monthly.

Standard Plus and Teams Plus

Who it fits: People who want meeting notes and email scheduling help without a separate AI tool.

What it adds: Calendly's Notetaker joins Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls, records them, and writes a recap with action items and a draft follow-up email. Callie, an AI assistant still in beta, joins your email threads to suggest times and book the meeting. Teams Plus also adds admin controls for these features. Calendly says both are only in English for now.

Price: Standard Plus is $18 a seat a month billed yearly ($24 monthly). Teams Plus is $24 a seat a month billed yearly ($32 monthly).

Calendly Enterprise

Who it fits: Large companies with security reviews, single sign-on and many seats.

What it adds: Everything in Teams, plus routing with Salesforce lookup, Microsoft Dynamics 365, SSO with SAML, SCIM user provisioning, domain control, audit logs, a data deletion API, phone support, a dedicated account team and help with setup. Notetaker and Callie are available on request.

Limits: It starts at $15,000 a year with a minimum of 50 seats, and you buy it through Calendly's sales team.

What the free plan leaves out

The Free plan is enough to try Calendly. For most working use, you'll miss these things:

  • More than one meeting type. You can't offer a 15-minute call and a 60-minute demo side by side.
  • More than one calendar. If you keep work and personal calendars apart, Calendly only checks one of them for conflicts.
  • Reminders and follow-ups. Automatic emails before and after the meeting start on Standard.
  • Group meetings. Group, collective and other shared meeting types need Standard.
  • Payments. Taking Stripe or PayPal payments at booking needs Standard.
  • Integrations. HubSpot, Mailchimp, Zapier, Google Analytics and Meta Pixel need Standard.
  • Your own look. Removing Calendly branding and setting your own embed colors need Standard.
  • Team features. Round robin and lead routing need Teams.

Extra costs to watch

Calendly's list price is only part of the bill. These are the costs people tend to find later:

  • Every host is a seat. If five people take meetings, you pay for five seats. A team of five on Teams pays $960 a year billed yearly, or $1,200 a year billed monthly.
  • Monthly billing costs more. Paying month to month adds $2 a seat on Standard, $4 on Teams and $6 to $8 on the Plus plans.
  • AI is a separate upgrade. Notetaker and Callie are not in Standard or Teams. Getting them means moving to a Plus plan.
  • SSO on Teams. Single sign-on is an optional add-on on Teams and only built in on Enterprise.
  • Enterprise has a floor. You can't buy Enterprise for a small team. It starts at 50 seats and $15,000 a year.
  • Paying by invoice. Calendly lists invoice payment on Standard or Teams at $5,000, and on Enterprise at $15,000. Otherwise you pay by card, or by PayPal if you're outside the US.
  • Renewals. Paid plans renew for the same period on their own unless you downgrade before the renewal date.
  • A second form tool. Calendly's routing forms start on Teams. If you want a full form before the booking on Standard, you may end up paying for a form builder too.

Calendly offers a 14-day free trial of paid features. At the end, your account drops back to the Free plan. Calendly also offers special pricing for qualified nonprofits through its support team.

Is Calendly worth it?

For most people who book meetings with clients or leads, Standard is worth the money. Unlimited event types, reminders and payments cover what a solo business needs, and $10 a seat a month billed yearly is a fair price for that.

Teams makes sense when leads are shared. Round robin and routing forms save a lot of back and forth, and the jump from Standard is $6 a seat a month billed yearly. Check the Plus plans only if you'd use the notetaker. If you already have a meeting recorder, they add cost without much gain.

Calendly is a weaker fit if your main goal is to turn website visitors into booked meetings. Its routing forms only start on Teams, so on Standard the form and the booking usually live in two tools. In that case it's worth looking at a tool that does both.

Questions

Is Calendly free?

Yes. The Free plan costs nothing and has no time limit. It includes one event type, one calendar connection and unlimited one-on-one meetings. Reminders, payments and more meeting types need a paid plan.

What are the limits of the Calendly free plan?

You get one event type, one connected calendar and one-on-one meetings only. You can't send automatic reminders, take payments, host group meetings, remove Calendly branding or connect HubSpot and Zapier.

How much does Calendly cost per month?

Standard is $12 a seat billed monthly or $10 billed yearly. Teams is $20 a seat billed monthly or $16 billed yearly. Standard Plus and Teams Plus cost $24 and $32 a seat billed monthly.

Does Calendly charge per user?

Yes. Calendly charges per seat, and a seat is a person who connects a calendar and hosts meetings. The people who book a time with you don't need a seat.

Does Calendly have a free trial?

Yes. Calendly offers a 14-day trial, and you can invite teammates to it at no cost. When it ends, everyone moves back to the Free plan.

Which Calendly plan has round robin?

Round robin starts on Teams, at $16 a seat a month billed yearly. Standard and Free don't include it.
