What is a form builder with conditional logic?

A form builder with conditional logic lets you set rules that change the form as people answer. A rule can show a question, hide it, skip a page or pick the ending. Each person only sees what applies to them.

Typical features include:

  • Rules that show or hide a question based on an answer.
  • Page skips that jump past whole steps.
  • Calculations that build scores and totals from answers.
  • Hidden fields that carry values like UTM tags into the rules.
  • Different endings or thank-you pages for different answers.
  • Logic on the emails sent after a form is filled in.

How to choose a form builder with conditional logic

Almost every tool says it has logic. Five checks show how much you really get:

  • Depth of logic. Rules that show or hide questions, plus jumps that skip whole pages.
  • Logic on the free plan. You should be able to test real rules before you spend money.
  • Calculations. Scores and totals built from answers.
  • What happens after the form. Where the answer goes once the logic has done its job.
  • Fair pricing. Clear plans with caps you can live with.

Its forms come with conditional logic and step skips on the free plan, plus calculations and formulas. When the logic decides a visitor is a fit, the same form books them with the right person.

2. Typeform

Typeform is an online form builder for marketing, product, HR, and customer teams that want forms, surveys, and quizzes people actually finish. It builds conversational forms that adapt to each answer, can draft and brand a form from a written prompt, and accepts video and audio replies.

Responses can go to tools like HubSpot, Google Sheets, and Notion, take Stripe payments, book Calendly meetings, and feed AI summaries of themes and sentiment.

Showing one question at a time makes branching feel natural. That suits brand-led forms where the look matters as much as the rules. The free plan has a low response cap, so most teams move to a paid plan quickly.

Key features

  • Conditional logic: Part of every plan from Basic up.
  • One question at a time: Keeps long branching forms easy to follow.
  • Typeform AI: Builds a form from a prompt and translates it into more than 25 languages.
  • AI answer analysis: Helps you read open-ended replies.

Pros

  • Smooth branching: The person filling it in never sees the questions they skip.
  • Design control: A good fit when the look of each question matters.

Cons

  • Tight response caps: 100 a month on Basic and 1,000 on Plus.
  • Free plan for trying out: It's mainly for testing the tool.

Price and plans

Conditional logic is on every plan, and the free plan includes 1,000 responses a month with unlimited forms and seats, so teams can build deep logic before they pay anything.

Key features

Cons

4. Jotform

Jotform is a drag and drop form builder for businesses, schools, nonprofits, and public agencies that want to collect data, payments, and signatures without writing code. Forms can use conditional logic, answer piping, prefilled fields, and translations, and they accept one-time or recurring payments through many gateways.

Submissions can become PDFs, flow into CRMs and spreadsheets, and be managed in related Jotform tools for tables, approval workflows, e-signatures, and reports. It is one of the oldest form builders, and its rules can show fields, skip pages, change emails and send people to different thank-you pages.

Jotform says conditional logic is on every plan, the free one included, which suits long forms like applications and intake forms with many branches.

Key features

  • Logic for free: Every plan includes it.

Price and plans

5. Tally

Tally is a form builder for individuals and teams who want to make forms by typing, much like writing in a document. You insert blocks for contact details, file uploads, ratings, signatures, and Stripe payments, then add conditional logic, calculations, hidden fields, and answer piping.

Forms can be embedded or shown as popups, and submissions can go to Notion, Slack, Airtable, or Google Sheets. Tally is hosted in Europe and does not use cookie tracking. Its free plan includes conditional logic and calculations with unlimited forms and submissions, within its fair use rules, so solo founders and small teams get logic for free.

Key features

  • Logic and calculations: Both on the free plan.
  • Unlimited use: Unlimited forms and submissions within fair use.
  • Answer piping: Reuse earlier answers in later questions.
  • Languages: Over 45, with right-to-left support.

Pros

  • Logic with no cap: Rules and math on a free plan with no submission cap.
  • Easy to build: Logic blocks sit right in the document.

Cons

  • Drop-off data costs more: Partial submissions and drop-off analytics need Pro.
  • Fewer native integrations: Many teams lean on Zapier or Make.

Price and plans

Forms can take bookings and Stripe payments, send contracts for e-signature through Papersign, and pass submissions to CRMs, email tools, and spreadsheets through integrations or Stepper workflows. Its forms look like web pages, with text and images between the questions.

Conditional logic is on every plan, and a calculations assistant comes on Pro and up. It works well for quotes, bookings and forms that need to read like a landing page.

Key features

7. Zoho Forms

Zoho Forms is an online form builder for businesses that want to collect data and move it through review and approval steps without code.

It can build a form from a prompt, turn PDFs or photos of paper forms into online forms, and read business cards or IDs to fill in fields. Forms support many field types, subforms, payments, and multi-level approvals, a mobile app collects entries offline, and data can flow into Zoho CRM.

Its rules are detailed. The pricing page lists field rules, form rules, page rules and set value actions, each with an advanced version. It fits best for teams already on Zoho CRM and other Zoho apps.

8. Gravity Forms

Gravity Forms is a form plugin for WordPress professionals and site owners who want to build anything from a simple contact form to a multi-step workflow on their own site. Its visual editor offers many field types, multi-page layouts, conditional logic, calculations and save and continue.

It can take payments through Stripe, Square and other processors, register users, create posts from entries, route notifications based on answers, and send data to other tools through a REST API and webhooks.

Conditional logic is a core feature on every license and works on fields and on the emails sent after a fill, with an add-on for rules that mix AND with OR. It suits WordPress sites that want entries stored on their own server.

Key features

  • Conditional logic: On every license.
  • Logic after the fill: On notifications and confirmation messages.
  • Advanced Conditional Logic: An add-on for grouped AND and OR rules.
  • HubSpot add-on: From the Basic license.

Pros

  • Your own data: Entries stay on your own server.
  • Logic everywhere: Rules work on fields and on the emails sent after.

Cons

  • No free version: And no trial.
  • WordPress only: It doesn't work on other site builders.

Price and plans

  • Basic: $59 a year.
  • Pro: $159 a year.
  • Elite: $259 a year.

Which form builder with conditional logic should you pick?

  • If you want the logic to end in a booked meeting with the right person, pick Claritia.
  • If you want the most free responses while you test your rules, pick Fillout or Tally.

Questions

What is conditional logic in a form?

Conditional logic is a set of rules that change the form based on answers. A rule can show a question, hide it, skip a page or pick the ending. People only see what applies to them.

Which form builders have conditional logic on the free plan?

Claritia, Fillout, Jotform, Tally and Paperform all include conditional logic on their free plans. Typeform includes it from its Basic plan. Check each plan's caps on submissions before you commit.

Does conditional logic make forms shorter?

Yes. Each person only sees the questions that fit their answers. A shorter path means fewer people give up halfway.

Can conditional logic do math?

Many builders pair logic with calculations. Claritia and Tally both include calculations on the free plan, so you can score answers and use the result in your rules.

Can a form route people based on their answers?

Yes. In Claritia, Pro plans route by any answer to a meeting and a person. A qualified lead can book with the right teammate before leaving the page.
