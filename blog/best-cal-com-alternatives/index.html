8 Best Cal.com Alternatives for Small Teams, Compared (2026)

Looking for Cal.com alternatives? Compare 8 hosted scheduling tools on round robin, routing, free plans and price, with honest pros and limits for each.

October 24, 20267 min read
On this pageHow we pickedThe quick comparison1. Claritia2. Calendly3. SavvyCal4. OnceHub5. Zcal6. Koalendar7. Zoho Bookings8. YouCanBookMeHow to chooseQuestionsTry Claritia

Cal.com does a lot right. Its source code is on GitHub, with guides for hosting it yourself. Its free plan gives one user unlimited links and calendars, with SMS notifications. For a developer or a solo user, that's hard to beat. The Teams plan adds round robin and routing forms at $12 a user a month.

People look elsewhere for a few common reasons. Some teams have no one to run or tune a scheduler and want everything handled for them. Others want the booking to start from a lead form, or want round robin without paying for every person on the team. This guide covers hosted tools that fit those needs, with the price of team scheduling shown up front.

How we picked

  • Round robin price: the plan and price where team scheduling starts.
  • Routing: whether answers can send a booking to the right person.
  • Free plan: what one person gets at $0.
  • Setup: how fast a small team can go live with no developer.
  • Switching: help moving bookings or event types over.

1. Claritia

Claritia joins forms and meetings for small teams, with workflows that follow up after. A visitor fills in your form and books inside it. Their answers decide which meeting they see and who on your team they meet.

Best for: small teams whose meetings start from a form on their website.

What stands out:

  • Route by any answer to a meeting and a person on Pro, with a routing log to see why each lead went where it did.
  • Round robin with weights and priority, plus daily and weekly limits for each person.
  • Only people others book with need a seat. Form builders and readers are free.
  • Starter takes in answers and bookings from Cal.com, which helps while you switch.

Where it falls short:

  • It's a newer tool, so its app marketplace and community are smaller than Cal.com's.
  • It's built for small teams more than large enterprises, and the Zapier and Make apps are still on the way.

Pricing: Free at $0, Starter $15 a seat a month ($12 billed yearly), Pro $39 ($31) and Business $79 ($63).

Pro also adds team pools and away days. Business adds owner-first routing from your CRM and double round robin. See Claritia and Cal.com and routing for the details.

2. Calendly

Calendly is the booking link most people already know. Clients rarely need help using it. Team features like round robin and lead routing sit on its Teams plan.

Best for: teams that want a familiar tool that's fully hosted.

What stands out:

  • A free plan with one event type and a booking page you can shape.
  • Standard adds unlimited event types and reminders.
  • Teams adds round robin and lead routing, plus Salesforce meeting sync.
  • Enterprise adds Salesforce lookup routing for larger teams.

Where it falls short:

  • The free plan has one event type and one calendar.
  • Round robin and routing need Teams at $16 a seat a month billed yearly.

Pricing: Standard $10 a seat a month billed yearly and Teams $16 billed yearly. Enterprise starts at $15,000 a year.

3. SavvyCal

SavvyCal is built around the person who books. Invitees can overlay their own calendar on your link to find a time. You can rank your open times so your favorite slots show first.

Best for: founders and consultants who book many calls with outside people.

What stands out:

  • Round robin and collective links on the Basic plan.
  • Ranked availability and frequency limits.
  • Reminders and workflows on every paid plan.
  • Premium adds your own domain and emails from your own address.

Where it falls short:

  • People can book you only on a paid plan.
  • The pricing page doesn't list routing by form answers.

Pricing: Basic $10 a user a month billed yearly ($12 monthly), Premium $17 billed yearly ($20 monthly).

4. OnceHub

OnceHub is a strong choice when routing is the main need. Its Route plan includes routing forms with booking inside the form. Engage adds chatbots that hand visitors to a live meeting.

Best for: sales teams that qualify leads with forms and chat.

What stands out:

  • Routing by form answers and contact details on Route.
  • Round robin, plus a mode that favors whoever has the most open time.
  • Chatbots with live chat and instant meeting handoff on Engage.
  • A free Basic plan for one person.

Where it falls short:

  • Routing and round robin start on Route at $19 a seat billed yearly.
  • SSO and HIPAA agreements are a paid add-on below Enterprise.

Pricing: Schedule $10 a seat a month billed yearly ($12 monthly). Route is $19 ($23) and Engage $39 ($47).

5. Zcal

Zcal's free plan is close to Cal.com's in spirit, with plenty for one person at no cost. Its Pro plan adds round robin at a low price. It's a good fit for solo users who may add a teammate later.

Best for: solo users who want a cheap path to team scheduling.

What stands out:

  • Unlimited links and calendar accounts on free.
  • Meeting polls and email reminders on free.
  • Pro adds round robin and collective events.
  • 750 SMS credits a month per user on Pro.

Where it falls short:

  • Zcal branding stays on the free plan.
  • Pro includes one team. More teams need Business.

Pricing: Pro $7 a user a month and Business $12 a user a month, with a 14-day free trial.

6. Koalendar

Koalendar keeps pricing simple for very small teams. Its Business plan bundles three seats with round robin. The free plan has unlimited booking pages and event types.

Best for: teams of two or three that want one flat price.

What stands out:

  • Unlimited event types on free, in more than 30 languages.
  • A one-click import from Calendly.
  • Business includes three seats with round robin and collective scheduling.
  • Business also adds a waitlist and SMS credits.

Where it falls short:

  • Reminders start on Pro, so the free plan sends none.
  • The pricing page lists more than one figure for its top tier, so confirm before you buy.

Pricing: Pro $6.99 a seat a month billed yearly ($9.99 monthly). Business is $29 a month billed yearly for three seats ($36 monthly).

7. Zoho Bookings

Zoho Bookings fits teams already on Zoho CRM or Bigin. It offers round robin with four ways to spread bookings. Its per-user prices are low on yearly billing.

Best for: teams that already run on Zoho apps.

What stands out:

  • A free plan for one person with two-way calendar sync.
  • Round robin with four distribution methods.
  • Ties to Zoho CRM and Bigin.
  • Premium includes three workspaces.

Where it falls short:

  • The free plan covers one person.
  • The pricing page doesn't make clear which plan includes round robin.

Pricing: Basic $6 a user a month billed yearly ($8 monthly), Premium $9 billed yearly ($12 monthly).

8. YouCanBookMe

YouCanBookMe gives you fine control over each booking page. You can set notice periods and protect pages with a password. Its Team plan adds round robin with one dashboard for everyone.

Best for: teams that want detailed settings on every page.

What stands out:

  • A free booking page with your logo and Stripe payments.
  • Minimum and maximum notice periods on paid plans.
  • Professional adds automated workflows and a HubSpot link.
  • Team adds round robin and roles for each member.

Where it falls short:

  • The free plan is capped at 30 bookings a month with a "Powered for free" button.
  • SMS costs extra on top of the plan.

Pricing: See their pricing page.

How to choose

  • If your meetings start from a form and you want the answers to pick the meeting and the person, pick Claritia.
  • If you want a hosted tool your clients already know, pick Calendly.
  • If you need routing plus website chat, pick OnceHub.
  • If you work alone today but expect to add a teammate, pick Zcal.
  • If you're a team of three and want one flat bill, look at Koalendar Business.

Questions

Why switch from Cal.com?

Teams often switch when they want a fully hosted tool with no setup work, or a different way to route and price team booking. Cal.com stays a good pick if you want to host it yourself.

Which Cal.com alternative has routing forms?

Claritia routes by any form answer on Pro. OnceHub includes routing forms on Route. Calendly includes lead routing on Teams.

Can you bring Cal.com bookings into Claritia?

Claritia's Starter plan takes in answers and bookings from Cal.com. That lets you run both while you move your pages over.

Which alternative is cheapest for round robin?

Zcal Pro adds round robin at $7 a user a month. SavvyCal includes it on Basic at $10 a user billed yearly. Koalendar Business bundles three seats for $29 a month billed yearly.

Do any of these need a developer to set up?

No. Every tool on this list is hosted for you, so setup takes a sign-up and a calendar connection.
