How to Build a Google Form With AI: 3 Ways That Work (2026)

Use an AI Google Form builder three ways: Gemini in Google Forms, a Forms add-on, or an AI form builder that sends every answer to Google Sheets.

Typing every question into Google Forms by hand is slow. You think up the questions, pick each field type, write the answer choices and fix the order. For a long survey or a quiz, that can take an hour before anyone sees the form.

AI can write the first draft for you in seconds. There are three ways to do it: Google's own Gemini inside Google Forms, an add-on from the Google Workspace Marketplace, or an AI form builder that sends every answer to Google Sheets the same way Google Forms does.

Try Claritia free to describe your form in one sentence, let Tia build it, and send each answer to Google Sheets.

In this post, we walk through each way step by step, from the prompt to a tested form with its answers landing in a sheet.

Main points

  • Google Forms has its own AI tool, "Help me create a form", powered by Gemini. It needs an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan.
  • Gemini drafts new forms and quizzes, but Google says it can't edit an existing form or build forms with several sections.
  • Add-ons such as Form Builder GPT add AI question writing to Google Forms itself.
  • AI form builders like Claritia, Fillout, Tally and Jotform build the form from a prompt and send answers to Google Sheets.
  • Pick Claritia if the form should also book a meeting. It can import a Google Form you already have, for free.

What you need

  • A Google account. For Google Forms, Google Sheets and most add-ons.
  • An eligible plan for Gemini. Google says "Help me create a form" needs an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan. Without one, use an add-on or another AI form builder.
  • A clear idea of the form. Who fills it in, what you need to learn and what happens next.
  • A Google Sheet, or a place for one. Each method below can put answers in a sheet.

Step 2: Write a clear prompt

AI drafts are only as good as the prompt. Say who the form is for, what it should collect and how long it should be. Name any choices you already know.

A weak prompt: "Make an event form."

A better prompt: "Create a registration form for a half-day product workshop. Ask for name, work email, company, job title, which of three sessions they want, and any dietary needs. Keep it under 8 questions."

Google's own help page gives the same tip: include more detail to get a better form.

Step 3: Build the draft with Gemini in Google Forms

Here's how Google's help page describes it:

  1. On your computer, open a new form in Google Forms.
  2. In the window that opens, or from the toolbar, click Help me create a form.
  3. Type your prompt and click Create.
  4. Read the draft. To try again, edit the prompt or click Recreate. Google warns that you can't go back to an earlier version after you recreate.
  5. When the draft looks right, click Create form.

The same tool makes quizzes. Gemini marks the correct answers and sets points, and you can check them with "Show answer key".

To add questions later, use Help me write a question or Suggest questions. Google says these need a form title and at least 2 questions, and they work in English on single-section forms.

Step 4: Or build it with a Google Forms add-on

If you don't have Gemini in Forms, add-ons bring AI to Google Forms from the Google Workspace Marketplace. You install one, open it from your form and it adds the questions it writes to that form.

Two examples:

  • Form Builder GPT by MyQuizGPT writes quizzes, surveys and registration forms from a prompt, a file or Google Drive content. You pick the questions to keep before they're added.
  • Form Builder imports questions from Sheets, Docs, Slides, PDFs, Word files and images, and its listing says it can write questions with AI as well.

Before you install one, read the permissions it asks for and its privacy policy. Add-ons are made by other companies, not by Google.

Step 5: Or build it in an AI form builder

An AI form builder drafts the whole form from your prompt, in its own editor. You then share it by link or put it on your site, and turn on its Google Sheets integration so answers land in a sheet.

In Claritia, it works like this:

  1. Sign up for free and go to Forms.
  2. Describe the form to Tia, Claritia's AI form builder, in one sentence.
  3. Ask Tia to add or fix questions, or edit them yourself.
  4. Add logic, a thank-you screen or a meeting to book at the end.
  5. On Starter, open the form's Sync tab, add Google Sheets and pick a sheet.

Already built a Google Form with Gemini? You can import it into Claritia by pasting its edit link. The questions come over, and your Google Form isn't changed.

Step 6: Add logic and sections by hand

AI drafts rarely get logic right on the first try. Check which questions should only show for some people, and set those rules yourself.

In Google Forms, logic works through sections: you send people to a section based on their answer. Gemini can't build forms with several sections, so add them after the draft.

In most AI form builders, you can ask the AI for a change or add show-if rules in the editor. Claritia includes conditional logic and page skips on every plan.

Step 7: Send the answers to Google Sheets

  • Google Forms: Open Responses, click More and choose Select destination for responses. Create a new spreadsheet or pick one you have.
  • Claritia: On Starter, each fill adds a row to the sheet you pick, and a new question gets its own column. See the Google Sheets integration.
  • Other tools: Fillout, Tally and Jotform each have a Google Sheets integration. Connect your Google account and pick the sheet.

Step 8: Test and share

Fill in the form yourself once, from a phone and a computer. Check that each answer lands in the right column and that any logic sends you down the right path.

Then share the link, embed the form on your site or send it by email. Watch the first few answers come in before you send it to everyone.

Common problems

  • The "Help me create a form" button is missing. Your account may not be on an eligible plan, or you may be on a phone. Google says it works on desktop.
  • Gemini replaced my form. Google says using it on a form with content replaces that form with the new draft. Start from a new, blank form.
  • "Suggest questions" doesn't appear. Add a title and at least 2 questions first. Google says it needs both.
  • The draft has too many questions. Ask for a set number in your prompt, or delete the extras.
  • Answers aren't showing in the sheet. Check that the sheet is still linked, or reconnect the integration in your form tool.

Tools for building a Google Form with AI

Google Forms with Gemini

Google Forms is the survey and form tool in Google Workspace, made for people and teams who want to gather answers from any device with little setup. You pick question types, arrange them with drag and drop, add logic that shows questions based on earlier answers, or build quizzes that test knowledge.

Forms can be shared by email, link, or embed. Responses appear as live charts and can be exported to Google Sheets for deeper analysis. With an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan, Gemini drafts new forms and quizzes from a prompt.

Claritia

Claritia is a forms, scheduling and follow-up app built for small B2B teams that want every lead from their website to reach the right rep and book a meeting before they leave the page.

It builds forms from a sentence or one of 840 templates, opens the right person's calendar the moment the form is sent, routes each lead by its answers, and drafts follow-up emails in your brand's voice for you to approve.

Tia comes with 100 AI credits on the free plan, and a whole new form uses 10. Google Forms import is free, and the Google Sheets integration starts on Starter at $12 a seat a month, billed yearly.

Fillout

Fillout is a form builder for teams that want branded forms, surveys, and quizzes connected to the tools they already use. You can describe a form for its AI to build or use drag and drop, then add logic, file uploads, payments, and signatures.

It also offers scheduling forms that sync with Google Calendar or Outlook, PDF generation, workflows, login forms that let people edit past answers, custom domains, and native integrations. Google Sheets is among the integrations on its free plan.

Tally

Tally is a form builder for individuals and teams who want to make forms by typing, much like writing in a document. You insert blocks for contact details, file uploads, ratings, signatures, and Stripe payments, then add conditional logic, calculations, hidden fields, and answer piping.

Forms can be embedded or shown as popups, and submissions can go to Notion, Slack, Airtable, or Google Sheets. Tally is hosted in Europe and does not use cookie tracking. Tally AI builds a form from a prompt, and its Google Sheets integration is free for all users.

Jotform

Jotform is a drag and drop form builder for businesses, schools, nonprofits, and public agencies that want to collect data, payments, and signatures without writing code. Forms can use conditional logic, answer piping, prefilled fields, and translations, and they accept one-time or recurring payments through many gateways.

Submissions can become PDFs, flow into CRMs and spreadsheets, and be managed in related Jotform tools for tables, approval workflows, e-signatures, and reports. Its AI Form Generator builds a form from a prompt, and its Google Sheets integration can add new rows or update existing ones.

Questions

Does Google Forms have AI?

Yes. Google Forms has "Help me create a form", powered by Gemini, which drafts a form or quiz from a prompt. Google says it needs an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan.

Can I use Gemini in Google Forms for free?

Not on a basic personal account. Google lists the feature as needing an eligible Workspace or Google AI plan. It's also offered through Google's Workspace Experiments tester program.

Can Gemini edit a Google Form I already made?

No. Google says "Help me create a form" can't edit an existing form, and using it on one replaces the form. You can add questions with "Help me write a question" or "Suggest questions".

What is the best AI form builder for Google Forms?

If you're on an eligible plan, Gemini is built in. On a free account, try an add-on. For customer-facing forms that also book meetings, build in Claritia and send answers to Google Sheets.

Can an AI form builder send answers to Google Sheets?

Yes. Claritia sends each fill to Google Sheets from Starter. Fillout, Tally and Jotform also connect to Google Sheets.

Can I move my Google Form to another tool?

Yes. Claritia imports a Google Form from its edit link on every plan, the free one included. The questions come over, and the answers stay in Google.
