How it works

Connect monday CRM in a few minutes

  1. 01

    Connect monday CRM

    Go to Settings, then Integrations, and paste your monday CRM API token.

  2. 02

    Pick it on a form

    Open the form's Sync tab, add monday CRM and match each question to a field.

  3. 03

    Publish

    New fills go to monday CRM as they come in, and the sync log shows each one.

Questions

Something else? hello@tryclaritia.com

Which plan has the monday CRM integration?

Starter and up, from $15/seat/month. On Free, fills can go out by webhook or CSV.

Does it make duplicate leads?

No. Claritia looks for the email in monday CRM first, so the same person is one lead.

What does Claritia send to monday CRM?

The fields you map in the form's Sync tab, like name, email, phone and company. Each fill in Claritia links to its lead.

What happens if monday CRM is down?

Every fill is saved in Claritia first. The send to monday CRM tries again on its own, and you can send it again from the sync log.
