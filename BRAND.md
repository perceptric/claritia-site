# Claritia brand: the website

**Version 2.0, 29 October 2026.** Replaces the warm-paper, serif and mint look of version 1.

Claritia sits in the same family as Omniscient (tryomniscient.com): black and white with plain grays, Inter,
and emerald as the one signal. What makes Claritia its own is the **corner bracket** from its mark. Things that
get caught (a lead, a form, the product itself) sit inside four brackets.

## The idea

**Catch every lead. Book the meeting.** The page shows one lead going from a visit to a booked meeting, and
lets the product do the talking.

## Mark

An emerald tile (`#0C8C5E`) with four white corner brackets and a check: caught, then confirmed. File:
`assets/brand/claritia-mark.svg`. The wordmark is Inter 600, sentence case. Never recolor, outline or shadow it.

## Color

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--ink` | #0a0a0a | Text, the one primary button, the open row |
| `--muted`, `--faint` | #6b6b6b, #a3a3a3 | Body and second lines |
| `--panel`, `--tint` | #ffffff, #f7f7f5 | Page and quiet bands |
| `--line`, `--line-strong` | #e7e7e4, #d4d4d0 | Hairlines |
| `--signal` | #0c8c5e | The mark, the caught lead, live lines, scores, booked |
| `--signal-text` | #0a7a52 | Green text on white |
| `--mint` | #18e299 | Green on black only |
| `--purple` | #5230c2 | The AI sparkle only |

About 5% of a view is emerald. Other hues only mean status. No gradients.

## Type

Inter for every word, Geist Mono for eyebrows, steps and numbers. Big headings take two tones: a line break
turns the rest gray (`.two`). Display 72px, section titles 48px, both with tight tracking.

## Shape

Buttons and tags are round pills. Controls 6px, cards 10px, frames 16px. Shadows only on frames and things that
float. The reliability section is the one black band, with bracketed cards.

## Pictures

- **The Catch (hero):** visitors drift in from every side; the ones that fit turn emerald. One lead runs the
  four corners (Capture, Know, Route, Book), the brackets snap, a check draws and "Booked" shows. 8 seconds, loops.
- **Product tour:** the real app drawn in HTML, in four parts with steps, a spotlight and a cursor.
- **Product drawings:** nine small line drawings, one per tool, in one stroke style: 1px lines, black for the
  thing, gray for the rest, emerald for what moves.
- **Closing mark:** the brackets and check draw themselves once.

## Motion

Three speeds (120, 200, 320ms) and two curves. Only transform, opacity and stroke offset move. Everything stops
under reduced motion and shows its last frame.

## Words

Short, plain and calm. Say what happens ("She is picking a time now"). No "·" between words in new copy, no
hype, no emoji.
