Questions

Something else? hello@tryclaritia.com

What is a seat?

A seat is a teammate people can book a meeting with. Teammates who only build forms, edit workflows or read results are free, so you only pay for the people who take calls.

What happens after 250 submissions on Free?

Your forms keep working and every submission is saved. Submissions past 250 show once you upgrade, or on the 1st of the next month. No lead is lost.

What is an AI credit?

One thing Tia does for you, like writing a form or adding questions. A small change is 1 credit and a whole new form is 10. When credits run out, Tia waits for the next month. Your forms, meetings and reminders keep running.

What if a paid plan passes its submissions?

Every submission is saved and shows right away. Additional form submissions are $5 per 1,000 on Starter and Pro. Business has no limit.

How does yearly billing work?

Pay for the year up front and save 20%. Monthly plans renew each month.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes, any time from Settings. An upgrade starts right away. A cancel takes effect at the end of the period you paid for.

What does Soon mean?

The feature is being built. It joins your plan when it ships, at no extra cost.

Do I need a card for Free?

No. Sign in with Google and start building.
